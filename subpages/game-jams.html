Banana Jam 3

This project started with a team of four and a month-long timespan to create a game. The goal of the jam was to challenge ourselves to learn something new, which I took full advantage of. During the process of determining what we wanted to make and how, we determined that we'd be using GameMaker. Prior to this jam, I'd never used GameMaker before and had no clue how it worked, so I spent a good portion of my time watching tutorials and reading documentation.

My goal for the project was to create both our character and background art as well as experiment with shaders to reduce the workload I'd have to do as an artist to create the desired visual effect.

My work led to me learning how shaders and post-processing function in GameMaker. GameMaker's shaders are in GLSL ES 1.0 unless changed by the developers, a language I also haven't used before. While the final effects were simple, they ended up being very useful in speeding up the art process.

I ended up with a shader that modifies the color of a sprite and a post-process that changes the saturation of sprites by a variable amount.
